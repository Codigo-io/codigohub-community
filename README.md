# Codigo Community Hub 🔥  

Welcome to the **Codigo Community Hub**!  

This is where our community can:  
- **Report bugs** 🐛  
- **Request enhancements** 💡  
- **Discuss the roadmap** 🚀  

## 🚀 How to Contribute  
1. Go to the [Issues](https://github.com/codigo-io/codigohub-community/issues) tab.  
2. Choose **Bug Report** or **Feature Request**.  
3. Fill in the details and submit!  

## 🌟 Community Guidelines  
Let’s keep it friendly and constructive. See [GitHub's Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) for more info.  

## 🌐 Explore CodigoHub  
Visit our platform at [codigohub.dev](https://codigohub.dev) to learn more and see Codigo in action!
